# Meridian Apparel, Inc.
## Prepaid Expense Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Finance Controls Team

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines when payments should be recorded as prepaid assets and amortized over the period of benefit.

---

## 2. Prepaid Review Threshold

Payments above **$5,000** that provide benefit beyond the current month should be reviewed for prepaid treatment.

---

## 3. Common Prepaid Items

Common prepaid items include:

- Annual software renewals
- Insurance premiums
- Maintenance contracts
- Subscription renewals
- Licensing fees
- Multi-month service arrangements

---

## 4. Review Requirements

When a transaction may require prepaid treatment, reviewers should confirm:

- Vendor
- Amount
- Service or benefit period
- Invoice date
- Whether the benefit extends beyond the current month
- Proposed amortization period
- Whether the prepaid schedule has been updated

A variance explanation is incomplete if it identifies an annual renewal but does not address prepaid treatment.
