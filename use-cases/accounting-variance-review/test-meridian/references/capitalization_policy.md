# Meridian Apparel, Inc.
## Capitalization Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Finance Controls Team  
**Applies to:** Fixed assets, IT hardware, durable equipment, furniture, fixtures

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines when Meridian records purchases as fixed assets instead of current-period expenses.

---

## 2. Capitalization Threshold

Purchases should be reviewed for capitalization when they meet both conditions:

1. The item has a useful life greater than one year
2. The purchase amount exceeds **$2,500**

Items that meet these conditions should generally be recorded as fixed assets unless there is a documented reason to expense them.

---

## 3. Common Capitalizable Items

Common examples include:

- Laptops
- Monitors
- Tablets
- Servers
- Store fixtures
- Furniture
- Durable equipment
- Point-of-sale hardware

---

## 4. Expense Treatment Exceptions

Expense treatment may be acceptable when:

- The item is below the capitalization threshold
- The item is consumable
- The item is a repair or maintenance expense
- The item does not create a future economic benefit
- The controller has approved a documented exception

---

## 5. Review Requirements

When a large equipment-related transaction is coded to an expense account, reviewers should confirm:

- The invoice detail
- The asset description
- The amount
- The expected useful life
- Whether the item exceeds the capitalization threshold
- Whether a fixed asset review was completed
- Whether a reclass entry is required
- Whether an exception was documented

A transaction should not remain in Office Supplies if it represents durable equipment above the capitalization threshold without support for expense treatment.
