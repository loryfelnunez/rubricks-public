# Meridian Apparel, Inc.
## Accounting Policy — Monthly Close Review

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Finance Controls Team  
**Applies to:** Monthly close, variance review, CFO package preparation

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines Meridian Apparel’s expectations for monthly close review, including variance explanations, account classification, support requirements, sensitive accounts, prepaid treatment, accrual review, and capitalization review.

The purpose of the close review is to ensure that monthly financial reporting is complete, supported, correctly classified, and ready for inclusion in the CFO close package.

---

## 2. Material Variance Review

A variance must be reviewed when it exceeds either of the following thresholds:

- **Dollar threshold:** $10,000
- **Percentage threshold:** 10%

A variance explanation must include:

1. The business driver of the variance
2. Whether the variance is timing-related or permanent
3. Whether the variance is supported by transaction detail
4. Whether account coding appears appropriate
5. Whether additional follow-up, reclass, accrual, prepaid, or capitalization review is required

A variance explanation should not merely restate that spending increased or decreased. It should explain why the variance occurred and whether the accounting treatment is appropriate.

---

## 3. Sensitive Account Review

The following accounts are considered sensitive and require additional review when variances exceed $5,000:

- Legal and professional fees
- Consulting
- Recruiting
- Executive expenses
- Travel and entertainment
- Unusual manual journal entries

Sensitive account variances require invoice support and reviewer sign-off before CFO package submission.

---

## 4. Coding and Classification Review

Expense coding must follow the Meridian Chart of Accounts & Expense Coding Guide.

Variances caused by unusual vendor activity, large transactions, new vendors, or transactions outside the normal run rate should be reviewed for possible miscoding.

Examples of coding issues to review:

- Durable equipment coded to Office Supplies
- Customer shipping coded to Inbound Freight
- Annual software renewals expensed fully without prepaid review
- Product samples coded to general office expense
- Legal invoices lacking sensitive account approval

---

## 5. CFO Package Readiness

Before a variance is included in the CFO close package, the preparer and reviewer must confirm that:

- The variance explanation identifies the actual driver
- Supporting detail is available
- Missing support is clearly called out
- Open follow-up items have an owner and due date
- Required reclass, accrual, prepaid, or capitalization review has been completed or documented

Unresolved items should not be presented as final.
