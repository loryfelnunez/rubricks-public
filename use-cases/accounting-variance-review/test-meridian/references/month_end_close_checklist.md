# Meridian Apparel, Inc.
## Month-End Close Checklist

**Period:** Standard monthly close  
**Owner:** Finance Operations  
**Used by:** Senior Accountants, Accounting Managers, Controller

---

## Day 1 — Close Start

- Confirm all subledgers are closed
- Confirm AP invoices through cutoff are entered
- Confirm bank and credit card feeds are refreshed
- Review unusual manual journal entries
- Identify missing recurring entries

---

## Day 2 — Account Review

- Review balance sheet accounts
- Review expense accounts above materiality threshold
- Review prepaid and accrual schedules
- Confirm fixed asset additions and disposals
- Identify unexplained or unsupported account movement

---

## Day 3 — Variance Preparation

For each material variance, preparers must provide:

- Account
- Department
- Actual
- Budget
- Forecast, if available
- Prior month actual
- Prior year actual
- Explanation of business driver
- Supporting transaction detail
- Owner for open follow-up
- Due date for unresolved items

---

## Day 4 — Controller Review

Before controller review, each material variance should include:

1. Clear driver
2. Supporting detail
3. Review status
4. Accounting treatment considerations
5. Missing support called out clearly
6. Required reclass, accrual, prepaid, or capitalization conclusion

A variance explanation is not ready for the CFO package if it is vague, unsupported, or fails to address an obvious accounting treatment issue.

---

## Day 5 — CFO Package

Before CFO package submission:

- All material variances must be reviewed
- Sensitive accounts must have support
- Unresolved items must include owner and due date
- CFO package must not include unresolved items as final conclusions
- Controller approval is required before distribution
