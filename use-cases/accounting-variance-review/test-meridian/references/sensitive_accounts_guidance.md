# Meridian Apparel, Inc.
## Sensitive Accounts Review Guidance

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Finance Controls Team

---

## 1. Purpose

This guidance defines accounts that require additional review due to sensitivity, judgment, or audit visibility.

---

## 2. Sensitive Accounts

The following accounts are sensitive:

- Legal and professional fees
- Consulting
- Recruiting
- Executive expenses
- Travel and entertainment
- Unusual manual journal entries

---

## 3. Review Threshold

Sensitive account variances above **$5,000** require additional support and reviewer sign-off.

---

## 4. Required Support

Sensitive account review should include:

- Vendor invoice
- Description of services
- Business purpose
- Approval evidence
- Reviewer sign-off
- Owner and due date for unresolved items

---

## 5. CFO Package Readiness

Sensitive account variances should not be included in the CFO package as final unless support is available and review status is clear.

If support is missing, the variance should be marked as unresolved and assigned an owner.
