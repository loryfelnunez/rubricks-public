# Meridian Apparel, Inc.
## Chart of Accounts & Expense Coding Guide

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Finance Controls Team  
**Applies to:** Expense coding, AP coding, variance review

---

## 1. Purpose

This guide helps accounting and AP teams classify expenses consistently across Meridian Apparel.

---

## 2. Account 6400 — Office Supplies

Use for low-value consumables such as:

- Pens
- Notebooks
- Printer toner
- Desk supplies
- Minor office consumables
- Low-value design supplies

Do not use for:

- Laptops
- Monitors
- Tablets
- Servers
- Durable IT equipment
- Furniture
- Store fixtures
- Equipment above the capitalization threshold

Large technology or equipment purchases coded to Office Supplies should be reviewed for fixed asset treatment.

---

## 3. Account 6200 — Marketing & Advertising

Use for:

- Digital advertising
- Agency fees
- Media placement
- Paid search
- Paid social
- Campaign production costs

Review for cutoff if the campaign runs across reporting periods.

---

## 4. Account 6600 — Software Subscriptions

Use for:

- Monthly software subscriptions
- SaaS tools
- Collaboration tools
- Analytics tools

Annual or multi-month renewals should be reviewed for prepaid treatment.

---

## 5. Account 5100 — Inbound Freight

Use for:

- Freight from supplier to warehouse
- Container delivery
- Supplier shipment charges
- Import freight

Do not use for:

- Customer shipping labels
- Ecommerce outbound shipping
- Fulfillment delivery to customers

Customer shipping activity should be reviewed for possible reclassification.

---

## 6. Account 7100 — Legal & Professional Fees

Use for:

- Outside counsel
- Contract review fees
- Litigation support
- Professional service retainers

Legal and professional fees are sensitive accounts. Variances require invoice support and reviewer sign-off.
