# AI Summary Review Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing AI-generated customer interview summaries.

## Accuracy
- Does the summary reflect what the customer actually said?
- Are claims backed by source evidence?
- Are direct quotes or concrete transcript details included where needed?

## Balance
- Does the summary preserve objections, hesitation, and constraints?
- Does it avoid making the customer sound more positive than they were?
- Does it include negative or mixed feedback?

## Reasoning
- Does the summary separate facts from interpretation?
- Does it avoid unsupported product recommendations?
- Does it identify uncertainty or confidence level?

## Completeness
- Does it include customer context?
- Does it explain the current workflow?
- Does it capture specific pain points?
- Does it include next steps or open questions?

## Actionability
- Are next steps specific?
- Is the recommended action proportional to the evidence?
- Does the summary identify what needs more validation?
