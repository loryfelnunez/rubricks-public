# Prior Research Themes

The BrightCart team has completed four previous discovery conversations with e-commerce operations users.

## Themes Seen So Far

### 1. Manual reconciliation creates timing risk
Teams often reconcile inventory data across Shopify, warehouse reports, and spreadsheets. This creates delays and missed reorder windows.

### 2. Alert fatigue is a major concern
Users are skeptical of generic alerts. They want alerts to be configurable, timely, and tied to business consequences.

### 3. Trust depends on data freshness
Users will ignore alerts if inventory data is stale or does not match what they see in Shopify or warehouse reports.

### 4. Reorder recommendations are more sensitive than alerts
Customers are more comfortable with alerts than automated reorder recommendations. Suggestions are useful, but users want control over final decisions.

### 5. Promotions change the threshold
Teams think about reorder timing differently before a promotion or seasonal sales event.
