# Customer Profile

**Customer:** Olivia Ramirez  
**Role:** Operations Manager  
**Company:** Harbor & Finch, a 22-person home goods e-commerce brand  
**Channels:** Shopify storefront, two wholesale accounts, seasonal pop-up events  
**Team structure:** Operations manager, inventory coordinator, part-time finance support, external warehouse partner

## Current Tools

- Shopify inventory exports
- Shared spreadsheet for reorder planning
- Email updates from warehouse partner
- Manual Slack reminders before promotions

## Current Pain

The team often catches inventory issues late because they reconcile data manually. The customer is interested in alerts, but only if the alerts are accurate, configurable, and not too frequent.
