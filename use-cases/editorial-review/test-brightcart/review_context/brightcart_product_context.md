# BrightCart Product Context

BrightCart is a fictional inventory operations platform for small and mid-sized e-commerce teams. The product helps teams monitor stock levels, identify replenishment needs, and coordinate purchasing decisions across operations, merchandising, and finance.

## Feature Under Consideration

The BrightCart product team is exploring a new feature called **Smart Inventory Alerts**.

The feature would notify users when inventory may need attention based on:
- Low stock thresholds
- Recent sales velocity
- Supplier lead time
- Upcoming promotions
- Manual overrides from operations teams

## Product Team Question

The team wants to know whether inventory alerts would solve a real customer problem or whether users would see them as noisy, unreliable, or hard to trust.

## Decision Under Review

The team is deciding whether to prioritize Smart Inventory Alerts in the Q3 roadmap.
