# Interview Goal

The customer interview was conducted to understand how small e-commerce teams currently manage replenishment decisions and whether automated inventory alerts would be useful.

## Research Questions

1. How does the customer currently track inventory levels?
2. What causes missed reorder timing or stockouts?
3. What signals would make an alert useful or trustworthy?
4. What would make alerts annoying or easy to ignore?
5. What decision would the customer expect an alert to support?

## Intended Output

The AI-generated summary should help the product team decide what to validate next, not prematurely commit to building the feature.
